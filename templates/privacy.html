Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register an account, as well as the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as login activity, to help keep your account secure.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the core functionality of Spendly: logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you filter and review your financial activity. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing practices. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database providers) solely to operate the Service, and these providers are bound to protect your data and use it only as necessary to provide their services to us.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
